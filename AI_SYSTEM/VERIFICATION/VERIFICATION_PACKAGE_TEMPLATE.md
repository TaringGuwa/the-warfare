9. VERIFICATION STOPPING CONDITIONS

Verification may stop when:

1. every requirement in scope has sufficient evidence;
2. every relevant acceptance criterion has sufficient evidence;
3. relevant contracts are verified;
4. relevant dependencies are verified;
5. no unresolved issue remains within scope.

Verification must deepen or escalate when:

- evidence is insufficient;
- evidence conflicts with Self-Check;
- requirements are ambiguous;
- semantic inconsistency is discovered;
- cross-system dependency exists;
- contract inconsistency exists;
- unexpected behavior is found;
- HIGH/CRITICAL issue is found;
- shared system is affected;
- regression is suspected;
- evidence indicates additional technically necessary scope.
